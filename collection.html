FORM / FUNCTION / SIMPLICITY

THE SILHOUETTE

This season is drawn in long lines and quiet volume. Shoulders sit softly. Hems fall with intention. The body is never overworked — only considered.

THE MATERIAL

Wool, linen, cotton and a single fine knit. Cloth is chosen for how it holds light and how it moves after years of wear — texture before ornament.

THE DETAILS

Buttons sit slightly off-centre. Seams are pressed, not announced. The work is in the places the eye does not immediately go.
